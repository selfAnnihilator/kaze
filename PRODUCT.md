# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Personal music listeners using Kaze as a desktop player to discover, organize, and play music without leaving their listening flow.

## Product Purpose

Kaze is a fully working desktop music player. Its interface should make discovery, library browsing, playlists, search, and playback feel immediate while preserving the established application behavior and navigation flow.

## Brand Personality

Calm, intimate, and atmospheric. The experience pairs a restrained Japanese-inspired editorial character with familiar desktop music-player interactions.

## Anti-references

Avoid loud neon streaming aesthetics, generic dashboard styling, decorative complexity that competes with music, and unfamiliar controls that obscure standard playback behavior.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the working product flow; presentation changes must not alter behavior.
2. Keep primary music actions familiar and immediately legible.
3. Let the atmospheric artwork establish mood without obscuring usable content.
4. Use hierarchy and spacing to keep discovery content visible at practical window sizes.
5. Prefer focused theme-layer refinements over structural rewrites.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, visible interaction states, keyboard-accessible native controls, and reduced-motion compatibility. Target WCAG AA where the existing architecture permits.
