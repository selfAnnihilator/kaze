# Kaze 2.0.4

## Fixes and improvements

- Keep playback identity, pause/resume and saved position when a track has unknown duration. Advance the queue on backend EOF, including short tracks, and disable duration-based seeking until a total is known.
- Load new accounts and library scopes without waiting for obsolete page requests. Preserve completed pages and offer retry after failures.
- Recover failed player-event registration with bounded retries, a persistent connection notice and an explicit Retry action.
- Keep album and local-search results tied to their account and destination. Show loading and retryable errors instead of stale playable rows.
- Refresh current-player and album artwork after metadata invalidation without restoring obsolete images.
- Improve playlist creation/membership recovery, interrupted downloads, startup behavior and account isolation.
- Update locked dependencies, release-workflow safeguards and Arch toolchain handling.

## Verification and known issues

The Linux checkout passed full automated tests, integrated checks in both interfaces, copied-binary native tests and rendering guardrails. The final acceptance audit records exact candidate fingerprints, test counts and reviewed quality findings.

The suspend/resume tray-Quit hang and long-session memory growth remain under investigation. Normal Quit tests do not establish resolution of the suspend-related issue. Windows/macOS runtime behavior, live-provider authentication and installer/update delivery require separate release checks.
