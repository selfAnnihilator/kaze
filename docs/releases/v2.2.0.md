# Kaze 2.2.0

## Kaze for Android (new)

- Kaze now runs on Android phones (arm64, Android 7 and later). The phone has its own interface built for touch: Home with Flow, Discover, Library and Queue tabs, a mini player that opens the full Deck, lyrics, and settings. Kaze uses the same account, playlists, likes and listening stats as the PC.
- Playback runs in the background, with lock-screen and notification controls, headset buttons, and pausing for calls and other apps.
- Songs download to `Music/Kaze`. Pick your music folders in Settings; Kaze asks for permission to read audio once.
- Pull down on Home or Discover to refresh. Swipe Discover chapters up and down, and swipe between albums in Library › Albums. Tapping an album card plays the album.
- Kaze checks for updates when it starts and from Settings › System & storage. It downloads the new version, checks it, and installs it (Android asks once to allow installs from Kaze).
- The app stays in portrait.

## Likes

- Songs from your own folders can no longer be liked or disliked, on PC or phone. Online songs and Kaze downloads can. Existing likes and dislikes on your own files are cleared once, and the heart and dislike buttons are greyed out on those songs.

## Fixes

- Seeking while a song had been paused for over a minute froze playback until Kaze restarted. It no longer does.
- On PC, Kaze re-read the downloads folder twice a second while open, using CPU even when idle. It now reacts only when files are added, removed or changed.
- Cloud sync uploads only what changed instead of the whole library every two minutes.
- A restored queue shows song names instead of "Queued track".
- Up Next follows the shuffled order, and starting a collection's shuffle always starts it shuffled.

## PC

- Screens, sections and pop-ups in the Brutalist interface animate in.
- The main window loads less code at startup.

## Verification

442 Rust tests and 166 Node tests passed, with clippy (PC and Android) and the conventions audit clean. Tested by hand on PC and on an Android 14 phone. No database migration; 2.1.1 data loads as is.
