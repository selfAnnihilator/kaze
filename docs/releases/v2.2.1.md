# Kaze 2.2.1

## Android

- Installing Kaze over data Android had restored from a backup could open already signed in, yet sync nothing: listening stats stayed at 0 and your likes and playlists didn't appear. Kaze no longer takes part in Android backups or phone-to-phone transfers, so a new install starts clean and asks you to sign in. If the saved sign-in can't be read, Kaze signs you out instead of looking signed in.
- The sync when Kaze opens no longer fails when other startup work is writing at the same moment, so your stats, likes and playlists are up to date as soon as the app opens.
- On the Profile's month chart, today is the bright bar, and a day you tap takes its place. Red marks only your busiest day.

If your phone shows 0 hours or is missing your likes, sign out and sign in again once.

## Verification

443 Rust tests and 171 Node tests passed, with clippy (PC and Android) and the conventions audit clean. Tested on an Android 14 phone: three launches in a row synced, and every Profile number matched the account's data. No database migration.
