# Kaze 2.0.3

## What's improved

- Use both themes in windows as small as 800 × 600. Artwork and spacing adapt to the available room, and content scrolls when needed to keep actions reachable.
- Keep Discovery's chapter gestures when content fits. When content overflows, scroll normally and use the chapter controls to switch sections.
- Get recommendations before building listening history. Choose artists and genres in a skippable setup after folder onboarding, or start with popular songs when catalog data is available.
- Update your choices in Settings → Music taste. Choices are saved separately for each account on this device; they do not sync across devices or create listening history.
- Start Flow and populate For You without first opening Trending or importing a local library. Existing feedback, recording deduplication and artist diversity still apply.

## What's fixed

- Pressing Play without a selected or queued song keeps playback stopped and shows “Select a song to play” for two seconds. Repeated presses refresh the same notice.
- Dislike confirmations dismiss after five seconds. Hovering or focusing the message pauses its countdown, and Allow again remains available. Dismissing the notice keeps the dislike saved.
- Preserve horizontal Discovery shelf positions when switching chapters or resizing the window.
