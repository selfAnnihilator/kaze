# ADR 0020: Prefer artist editions without swapping recording artwork

The reported Sia Breathe Me cover was the artwork supplied for the saved Night Train compilation edition (`uegtrjK9`, album `78973062`). Live album metadata credits Various Artists. The service also supplies Sia's Breathe Me single and Colour The Small One editions. The artwork was attached to the selected compilation correctly; the selection policy made that compilation unnecessarily sticky.

Search now keeps provider relevance between songs while ranking editions of the same title/artist together. Singles, explicit album queries and older equivalent releases receive preference over recent compilation duplicates. All alternatives remain addressable by their own IDs. Search sorting uses the existing response and adds no per-song network requests.

Discovery checks at most three current/recent saved online recordings concurrently for artist-owned alternatives, with a five-second timeout per check and an hour-long bounded attempt cache. Album credits must explicitly identify the source as Various Artists before a targeted song lookup. An alternative must match title, primary artist and duration, pass the existing full-audio availability checks, and have album credits naming that artist. Unavailable/ambiguous alternatives leave the compilation available. These requests happen during discovery preparation, outside playback/next-track resolution and progress updates.

`catalog_artist_editions` persists verified source-to-preferred-source evidence. Discovery and Flow use that evidence ahead of album-name heuristics. Flow no longer exempts listened/liked online editions from release comparison: song-level statistics keep the artist edition familiar, instead of forcing the compilation merely because it was heard first. Local files retain their existing preference and are not replaced. Discovery invalidates the user's cached Home picks after ingesting alternatives.

Provider persistence now saves the complete fetched source metadata, including album ID, year and language. Earlier direct catalog binding reconstructed a reduced record and could discard album identity needed for later release checks. Existing source snapshots remain immutable; fresh artist editions receive separate IDs, their own artwork and their own audio/download source. Saved playlists and an already selected/queued compilation keep that edition. Re-searching/playing the song or starting a fresh generated selection uses the new preference. Artwork from another edition is never pasted over an existing recording.

Regression coverage failed before the fix for compilation-first search and a liked/listened compilation in Flow. Added integration coverage requires album-credit evidence even when title/year heuristics cannot distinguish the alternatives, preserves both source snapshots and covers, checks full release metadata, and verifies cached checks avoid extra requests. The opt-in Sia live gate verifies the actual artist edition's cover/album ID against exact detail metadata, decodes its 500×500 cover and downloads/decodes the same recording as M4A in disposable storage. Final verification is recorded in the dated experiment checkpoint.
